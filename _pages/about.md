I am a PhD student at the [International Centre for Theoretical Sciences](https://www.icts.res.in/) (ICTS-TIFR), Bengaluru, India, where I joined in August 2022. I study plasma flows around black holes: how turbulence and magnetic fields drive accretion, and how they are connected to the launching of jets. My main tool is large-scale general relativistic magnetohydrodynamic (GRMHD) simulations, and I enjoy the process of building, running and analysing them. I am also interested in connecting simulations to observations through polarimetric signatures, for example around M87\*.

From January to May 2026 I was a predoctoral researcher at the Center for Computational Astrophysics, Flatiron Institute, in New York.

Before ICTS, I completed an MSc in Physics at Kalasalingam Academy of Research and Education and a BSc in Physics at The Gandhigram Rural Institute, both in Tamil Nadu, India.

Beyond research, I convened the ICTS Astro-Club, help with public outreach and telescope sessions, and take [photographs]({{ "/photography/" | relative_url }}).

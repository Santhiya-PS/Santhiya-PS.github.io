## Interests

## Projects

### Dynamo and jet interconnections in GRMHD simulations of black hole accretion disks

*The Astrophysical Journal*, 1011(1), 2026. [DOI: 10.3847/1538-4357/aea4b5](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aea4b5) · [arXiv:2512.02129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02129)

With Pallavi Bhat, Prayush Kumar, Tushar Mondal and Indu K. Dihingia.

Magnetic fields play a central role in how black holes accrete matter and launch jets. This project uses global 3D GRMHD simulations to study how a dynamo driven by the magneto-rotational instability generates large-scale magnetic fields in the accretion disk, and how those fields connect to the launching and longevity of the jet.

### Unresolved magnetic field structure and its impact on polarization signatures of the accretion flow around M87\*

*In progress*

With Alisa Galishnikova and Lorenzo Sironi.

Simulations cannot resolve the smallest scales of the magnetic field in the accretion flow. This project asks how that unresolved structure changes the polarized emission we would observe from M87\*.
